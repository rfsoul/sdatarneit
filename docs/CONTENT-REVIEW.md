## Individual arrangements

SDA accommodation and daily/overnight support services are distinct. No staffing roster, SIL provider, funding outcome or individual suitability is promised. Participants, families, support coordinators and SIL providers may enquire before funding or support arrangements are finalised.

Do not invent accessibility specifications, equipment installations, travel times, shopping distances, prices or inclusions. The owner and Disability Forever Homes can coordinate inspection and property suitability questions; visitors can discuss funding and support with their own team.

## Enquiry privacy

The form saves a timestamped JSON file with a unique reference to private persistent server storage. It does not open an email app. After a successful local save, a separate server job emails a plain-text notification and JSON copy to the owner through Resend. Visitors may alternatively email `enquiries@sdatarneit.au`; Cloudflare Email Routing forwards those messages to the owner's Gmail, but direct email messages are not saved as website enquiry files. The privacy page explains both routes, processing, use by the owner, potential coordination with the property manager, manual retention and access/correction/deletion requests. Visitors are asked not to submit sensitive records.
