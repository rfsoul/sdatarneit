# Items to confirm before making more specific claims

- Confirm the current property enrolment details and whether any non-HPS category or resident configuration is approved. Current public copy invites a conversation only and does not promise a match.
- Confirm exact measurements and access details (door clearances, turning spaces, thresholds and entry route) before publishing dimensional or step-free claims.
- Confirm which equipment and assistive technology are installed or included, and any relevant power or backup provisions, before describing them as available.
- Recheck availability and the furnishing of each bedroom before each major content refresh.
